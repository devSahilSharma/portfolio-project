# Personal Site

The owner's personal brand website: where visitors see the owner's work, read their writing, and reach out about opportunities.

## Language

### Work

**Experience**:
A job the owner has held at a company: company, title, dates, what they did there, and the Stack they used. Mirrors the Résumé's work history.
_Avoid_: Job, work history, career

**Stack**:
The technologies used in a single Experience or Project, listed alongside it. Skills are shown only this way, never as a standalone list.
_Avoid_: Skills section, tech list

**Project**:
Something the owner built that a visitor can open themselves, via a live demo, source code, or both.
_Avoid_: Portfolio item, work sample

**Résumé**:
A downloadable PDF supplied by the owner. Covers the same work history as Experience, plus achievements and other details the site doesn't show.
_Avoid_: CV

### Writing

**Post**:
A single piece of the owner's writing, on any topic, published under the site's own domain.
_Avoid_: Article, blog, entry

### Reaching out

**Contact links**:
The owner's email and social profiles, through which a visitor reaches out.
_Avoid_: Contact form
